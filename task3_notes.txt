1. Relationship Type:
   Customers to Sales: One-to-Many (1:*) relationship using CustomerID.
   Products to Sales: One-to-Many (1:*) relationship using Product (or ProdID).

2. Total Sales Calculation:
   Total Sales is calculated using SUMX(Sales, Sales[Quantity] * Sales[UnitPrice]) to sum row-by-row item totals across all transactions.

3. Key Business Insights:
   - Category Performance: Electronics and Accessories drive the highest total revenue.
   - City Performance: Major revenue shares come from top cities like Lahore and Karachi.
   - Monthly Trend: Sales remained steady across the evaluated timeframe.
